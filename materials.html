Materials & technology

Understand waterproofing material categories and how system selection depends on site conditions.

◈

Polyurethane systems

Selected injection, joint and coating applications. Suitability depends on product chemistry, moisture and exposure.

◈

Epoxy systems

Selected crack injection, bonding and flooring applications. Not generally suited to moving or wet cracks unless the product permits.

◈

Cementitious waterproofing

Cement-compatible coating systems for selected concrete and masonry substrates.

◈

Acrylic waterproofing membranes

Selected exposed roof and wall applications; verify ponding-water suitability.

◈

Polymer modified coatings

Protective and waterproofing coatings selected for substrate and exposure.

◈

Protective PU coatings

Selected surfaces requiring a protective coating with appropriate preparation.

◈

Joint sealants

Flexible sealing systems for joints and interfaces with movement.

◈

Injection grouting materials

PU, epoxy or other grout systems chosen for the leakage path and application.

◈

Reinforcement mesh

Used in compatible systems to reinforce selected details and transitions.

◈

Waterproofing membranes

Sheet or liquid-applied membrane systems selected for the project.

◈

Thermal insulation coatings

Roof coating options intended to help manage solar heat gain; performance depends on the roof assembly.

Specific products, specifications and manufacturer recommendations must be checked against current technical data sheets. No manufacturer authorisation or brand partnership is implied.
