Warranty Policy

How project-specific warranty terms are confirmed.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Written confirmation

A warranty is valid only when explicitly offered in writing in the accepted quotation, invoice or work order.

Project conditions

Any warranty may depend on the defined treatment area, substrate condition, preparation, product system, workmanship, maintenance and site exposure.

Exclusions

Structural movement, plumbing failures, third-party alterations, physical damage, lack of maintenance, new leakage sources and conditions outside the agreed scope may be excluded where stated in the project document.

No blanket promise

This website does not promise a fixed warranty period or guaranteed leak-free result for every service.

Claims

Contact the business with the project reference, date and a description of the concern. The accepted project document will govern the review.
