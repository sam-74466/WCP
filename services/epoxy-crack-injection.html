Epoxy Crack Injection

Pressure injection of suitable epoxy resin into selected concrete cracks where structural bonding is appropriate and conditions permit.

Structural Repair & Injection Systems

A treatment plan based on site conditions.

Pressure injection of suitable epoxy resin into selected concrete cracks where structural bonding is appropriate and conditions permit.

Common problems

Cracks in concrete requiring bonding, dry or suitably prepared crack paths and localised structural repair.

Typical applications

Selected RCC members after engineering assessment.

Inspection and methodology

Evaluate crack width, moisture, movement and structural significance. Epoxy injection is not suitable for every leaking or moving crack.

  • Review the affected area and surrounding details
  • Identify likely sources and substrate conditions
  • Confirm suitability of the proposed treatment
  • Prepare the surface and apply the selected system
  • Carry out appropriate checks and hand over the work scope

Epoxy Crack Injection – FAQs
